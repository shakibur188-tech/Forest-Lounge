BEFORE YOUR VISIT

Good to know.

Do I need to reserve a table?

We recommend sending a reservation request, especially for groups and special occasions. Our team will contact you to confirm availability.

What are your opening hours?

Every day · 12:00 PM – 11:00 PM

Can I plan a private event or party?

Yes, you can send an enquiry for birthdays, family celebrations, or corporate gatherings. Tell us your preferred date and group size using the booking form.

Where can I see the menu and buffet prices?

Explore our printed à la carte menu above. Please call our team for the current buffet selection, prices, and availability.

Can you accommodate dietary requirements?

Please include any dietary requirements or allergies in your reservation request and speak to our team before ordering.
